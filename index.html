Why Beside?

Because needing a little help doesn't mean giving up independence.

We're exploring a service that sits somewhere between family, friendship and practical everyday support.

For seniors

Need someone to pick up your medicines? Want company on a walk? Need help with shopping, technology, appointments or simply want someone to spend some time with?

For families

Living in another city or country doesn't mean you stop worrying about your parents. We're exploring a way to give families a trusted person nearby when they can't personally be there.
